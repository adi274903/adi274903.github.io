Robot arm pick-and-hand-over

Robotics hackathon, 2026, Robot48hrs hackathon project

9 of 24logged live runs lifted the object, mostly tuning runs on the last day

What I built

  • Built a Viam robot-arm pick-and-hand-over system at a 48-hour hackathon: YOLO and a depth camera locate a cup or bottle, Viam's motion service re-plans short collision-aware moves each step, the arm pauses when a person is close, and every run is logged to MongoDB with a failure class (9 of 24 logged live runs lifted the object).
  • The arm hands the object over only after the operator presses Yes on a web control page, STOP is a clean stop, and the gripper, which has no force limit, closes by position to a width set per object (cup 70 mm, bottle 45 mm).
  • Of the 24 live runs logged on the last day, 9 lifted the object (bottle 4 of 6, cup 5 of 18); the other 15 were 9 operator stops, 3 errors and 3 other failures. These were mostly tuning runs, not a benchmark.

A robot arm, a depth camera and Viam's motion planning, built in 48 hours: it finds a cup or bottle, picks it up and hands it over once a person says yes.
