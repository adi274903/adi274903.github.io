What I built

  • Delivered Med-Qwen-3.5-4B-Instruct, a 4.2B clinical reasoning SLM that outscored MedGemma-1.5-4B-it on all six clinical benchmarks (PubMedQA 66.20, MedQA USMLE 67.95), by domain-adaptive pretraining over Qwen3.5-4B-Base then a 2,700-step DoRA (r=64, alpha=128) supervised fine-tuning pass over an 11-stream clinical reasoning mixture (~276M tokens).
  • Scored PubMedQA 66.20 against 45.60 for Qwen3.5-4B-Base, with GPQA Diamond 50.51, MMLU Med 80.72, MedMCQA 33.20 and AfriMed-QA 64.80 under zero-shot greedy inference with normalized choice log-probabilities.
