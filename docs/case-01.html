What I built

  • Built Med-Qwen-3.5-4B-it in three stages on Qwen3.5-4B-Base: domain-adaptive pretraining, supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO), released as one merged checkpoint on Hugging Face.
  • Scored 73.5% on MedQA (USMLE) and 75.2% on PubMedQA at about 66 generated tokens per answer, where the other recent small models in the comparison need 750 to 4,100 tokens for similar MedQA accuracy.
  • Cut medical reasoning length about 7x (456 to 66 tokens) at matching MedQA accuracy by training a GRPO RLVR adapter on Qwen3.5-4B with rule-based accuracy, format and length rewards.

A 4B clinical reasoning model that beats MedGemma-1.5-4B on all five benchmarks I ran, built in three stages on Qwen3.5-4B-Base: domain pretraining, fine-tuning, then reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO).

Generative evaluation: each model reasons, then answers, with greedy decoding, one shared prompt and parser, in my own harness. Most differences sit inside the 95% confidence intervals, so near-ties are ties, and GPQA Diamond favours models that answer briefly. Benchmark overlap: 0.03% of the RLVR prompt pool matched a benchmark question verbatim; the SFT and DAPT data were not fully audited. Source: the Med-Qwen-3.5-4B-it model card.
