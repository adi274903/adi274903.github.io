NCM-MoE Omni training study

Training study, 2026, Personal project

170 TFLOPSsustained on one RTX PRO 6000 over a 2B-code run; MFU about 34% against an assumed peak

What I built

  • Trained a from-scratch 1.68B sparse mixture-of-experts (about 364M active per token, 6 experts) on text, image and audio codes on one RTX PRO 6000, sustaining about 170 TFLOPS (about 65k codes/s) over a 2,006-step, 2B-code run.
  • Cut the audio codec's cost by switching DAC from 44.1 kHz to 16 kHz: encode time per micro-batch fell from 0.690 to 0.262 seconds and throughput rose from 145 to 170 TFLOPS.
  • Text and image learned (held-out CE 3.34 and 1.76 against baselines of 4.90 and 4.12); audio overfit, with training CE 1.001 against held-out 1.775 and held-out audio CE rising from 1.52 at step 753 to 1.78.
  • Published as a training study, not a model: MFU about 34% against an assumed 500 TFLOPS peak, the single-factor ablations, what broke, and what I would change with 8 GPUs. No weights are released.
