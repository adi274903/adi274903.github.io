Ask my record

Ask about my work.

Ask about my projects, results, roles or papers, or which of my work fits a fintech, healthtech or AI engineering role.

  1. Chunk. My record is cut into fifteen sections, one per project, role or topic, split into forty-one short lines. Each section carries metadata: project, organisation, year, domain, topics and tools.
  2. Match. No AI model runs here. Each question and each line becomes a TF-IDF vector; names such as EMMA or Tata pick the section, metadata filters pick projects by topic or role, and cosine similarity picks the best lines.
  3. Quote. A fixed template writes the reply around those lines. Same question, same answer, every time.

It refuses anything outside my record, such as coding help or general questions. It was checked on fifty-six questions I wrote; every set was used to tune it, so treat that as a regression check, not a blind test. With this panel open, each answer also shows its similarity score and metadata tags.
