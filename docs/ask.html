Ask my record

Ask about my work.

No AI model runs here. Your question is turned into a vector and compared, by cosine similarity, with fifteen sections of my record, split into forty-one short lines. The best match is quoted back to you, with its source, so every answer is something I wrote about my own work.

How it works
  1. Chunk. My record is cut into fifteen sections, one per project, role or topic.
  2. Match. Each question and each line becomes a TF-IDF vector. Names such as EMMA or Tata pick the section; cosine similarity picks the best lines inside it.
  3. Quote. A fixed template writes the reply around those lines. Same question, same answer, every time.

It refuses anything outside my record, such as coding help or general questions. It was checked on fifty-one questions I wrote; the first twenty-nine were used to tune it, so treat that as a regression check, not a blind test.
